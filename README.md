E3SM Land Model with implicit mycorrhizal function (ELM-MYCI)
================================================================================

This is a branched version of the Energy Exascale Earth System Model (E3SM), a state-of-the-art fully coupled model of the Earth's climate including important biogeochemical and cryospheric processes. E3SM is intended to address the most challenging and demanding climate-change research problems and Department of Energy mission needs while efficiently using DOE Leadership Computing Facilities. The main branch of E3SM is here: https://github.com/E3SM-Project/E3SM

The ELM-MYCI branch adds implicit representations of ecto- and ericoid mycorrhizal symbionts into its boreal needleleaf trees and boreal shrubs plant functional types. It is intended to represent the important roles of these mycorrhizal associations in plant nutrient access, especially from the soil organic matter pools.

The associated manuscript is at Wang et al. (2026) Implementing belowground controls on nutrient uptake in ELMv2-SPRUCE improves representation of a boreal peatland ecosystem, Geosci. Model Dev., 19, 5827–5855, https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-19-5827-2026
